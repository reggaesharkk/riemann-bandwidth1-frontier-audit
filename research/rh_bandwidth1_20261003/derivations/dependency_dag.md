# Alpöge–Furman dependency map

Prime-side analytic estimates
→ finite matrix trace and Hilbert–Schmidt moments
→ on-line positivity plus off-line-pair inertia
→ rank–trace inequality and multiplicity ledger
→ simple-on-line / distinct-zero density.

## Analytic inputs → matrix moments

For a window ψ supported in [-1/2,1/2], let L≈log T and X=e^L≤T. The test function φ is a smoothed square root of ψ(u/L) with support [-L/2,L/2]. Products of modulated copies have Fourier support in [-L,L], the bandwidth-one range. Weil's explicit formula turns the finite compression into a prime-side mean.

Unconditional analytic inputs listed in AF §1.1/§5:
- Weil explicit formula;
- Riemann–von Mangoldt and unit-interval zero counting;
- Stirling estimates for Γ′/Γ;
- Chebyshev–Mertens estimates for sums of Λ(n)^2 and Λ(n)^2/n;
- Montgomery–Vaughan for frequencies log n, with X≤T.

These yield tr(G̃)=(1+o(1))N and tr(G̃²)=||G̃||HS²=(R(ψ)+o(1))N.

## Zero side → inertia

The compression decomposes, up to a negligible tail, as G̃=P+Q. P≥0 is generated by on-line zeros. Each off-line pair contributes a real symmetric block of signature (1,1), giving n₊(Q)≤number of off-line pairs. Multiplicity counts constrain N, tr(P), and the positive index.

For simple-on-line zeros, use P₁ equal to the simple-on-line part of P and Q′=G̃−P₁. Then P₁≥0 and n₊(Q′) is bounded by the residual off-line/multiple-zero count. The finite matrix inequality
rank(P₁) ≥ 2 tr(P₁)+4 tr(Q′)−4b−||P₁+Q′||HS²
produces the density coefficient.

## Output constants

- Simple-on-line: 2−R(ψ).
- Distinct zeros: (3−R(ψ))/2.
- Indicator window: R=4/3, giving 2/3 and 5/6.
- Montgomery–Taylor window: R=1/2+(1/√2)cot(1/√2), giving C0=2−R and C1=(3−R)/2=(1+C0)/2.

## Legality boundary

At X≈T, the unconditional evaluation supplies the first trace moment and the Hilbert–Schmidt (second) moment. AF §7.2 states that the diagonal method gives higher tr(G̃^k) only when X^k≤T^(2−ε); at X≈T this does not permit k≥3. Any optimization that uses a higher moment or support beyond 1 must label the extra input conditional.
