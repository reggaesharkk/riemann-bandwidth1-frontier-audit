# WP84 — candidate theorem priority and targeted literature comparison

This is a conservative triage for external independent review, not a novelty search and not a publication recommendation. The ratings compare the six isolated candidates in the frozen theorem inventory. A score of 5 is strongest within this small set; it is not an absolute journal-readiness score.

## Targeted literature comparison

| Candidate ingredient | Established literature context | Consequence for the WP84 extraction |
|---|---|---|
| Fourth cumulant as a partition sum | Speed, “Cumulants and Partition Lattices 1” develops joint cumulants through Möbius inversion on the partition lattice. | The 26-term formula and its Möbius signs are classical. The WP84 contribution is matching its ordered/anchored convention to the source rows, not discovering cumulants. |
| Schatten-4 perturbation | Schatten ideals and their norm inequalities are standard trace-ideal theory; see Simon, *Trace Ideals and Their Applications*, 2nd ed. | The ℓ4 triangle step and (\tau(Q^4)\le\|Q\|_{op}^2\tau(Q^2)) are standard. The source-specific work is proving the proper-power operator and grid-trace bounds under the actual cutoff. |
| Separated-frequency square mean | Montgomery–Vaughan, “The Large Sieve,” gives the separated-point inequality and its dual. | The large-sieve mechanism is classical. It applies to the verified one-prime proper-power frequency grid; it does not automatically control the missing two-prime product-frequency endpoint score. |
| Pair-layer Fourier calculation | Toeplitz symbols and Parseval trace calculations are classical; see Grenander–Szegő, *Toeplitz Forms and Their Applications*. | The exact R=400 covariance contraction is a specialized finite calculation under WP84 covariance inputs, not a new Toeplitz theorem. |
| Fixed-scale Brownian/min kernel | (\min(s,t)) is the Brownian covariance and has the elementary indicator-integral Gram representation; the Schur product theorem is standard matrix analysis. | The PSD step is classical. The six-orientation chamber identity is the specialized algebra to audit; neither establishes a physical-kernel identity. |
| Endpoint variation and finite-section tensor | Total-variation estimates, telescoping products, and finite Fubini expansion are elementary. | The source-specific term ledger is useful reproducibility work, but the abstract identities do not appear publication-grade without a nontrivial new application. |
| Physical/geometric mismatch | No targeted literature source was found that states this exact WP84 comparison. | This is a verified source-specific counterexample to a kernel identification. It is not evidence of novelty; it is small enough that priority should not be claimed without broader literature review. |

## Genuinely self-contained vs source-dependent

- **Self-contained after definitions:** physical/geometric finite witness; endpoint total-variation bound; tensor identity; geometric fixed-scale model theorem and Gram factorization. The chamber identity is supported by an exact finite checker; a publication draft should expose the complete chamber table or a compact symbolic proof.
- **Self-contained with explicit source hypotheses:** proper-power Schatten absorption. The theorem does not need an ordinary-prime fourth-moment bound, but its WP84 hypotheses and normalization must be matched independently.
- **Conditional on source covariance inputs:** fixed-band pair-pair formula and the (R=400) rational-π certificate.
- **Not established:** complete product-frequency selection, the ordinary-prime connected aggregate, the record-budget implication with all consumer conditions, either percentage claim, and RH.

The targeted comparison checks that each simple mechanism has standard precedents. It is not an exhaustive review, does not establish priority, and should not be cited as a novelty determination.
