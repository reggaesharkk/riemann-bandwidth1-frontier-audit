# WP71 / WP80 budget firewall

Date: 2026-10-04
Status: **MANDATORY EVIDENCE-SEPARATION RULE**

Two numerical margins currently coexist in the project, but they belong to different proof objects and must never be spent against one another.

## WP71 Stage-A bridge margin

WP71 concerns the fourth-moment shifted-correlation remainder R and the route to the exact continuum core C_core=-1/48. Its unresolved collar, glue and remainder terms must be shown to be o(1) in the exact WP71 R-normalization (or otherwise bounded by the relevant WP71 threshold).

## WP80 one-sided m7/m8 corridor

WP80 concerns the degree-4 one-sided moment consumer beyond 80%. For a transported m7 lower bound L, its feasible m8 corridor is

    T80(L)-F8(L)
      = (2666160/10471)*(L-L0)^2.

At L=19.136 this width is about 0.02373565.

That number is a **moment-space m8 slack**. It is not an R-unit error budget.

## Firewall rule

Do not claim that a WP71 collar/error is acceptable merely because it is numerically smaller than the WP80 corridor 0.02373565.

The valid closure conditions are separate:

- WP71 error -> prove o(1) or satisfy the exact WP71 R threshold in WP71 units.
- WP80 m8 error -> prove U-F8(L) is below the exact T80(L)-F8(L) corridor in moment units.

Only after the analytic moment transport itself is proved can the WP80 corridor be consumed.

This rule prevents cross-unit budget leakage between the 71.164% Stage-A theorem route and the post-79 one-sided moment route.
