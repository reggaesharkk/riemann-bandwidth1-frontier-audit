This note tests whether that energy can be forced into the exponentially amplified endpoint interval by positivity alone.\n\nIt cannot.\n\n## 1. Equal-depth configuration\n\nFix `0<theta<1/4`; for concreteness take `theta=1/8`. Let\n\n    J=[(1-2theta)L,(1-theta)L].\n\nTake positive multiplicities `m_j=1`, equal depths, and ordinates\n\n    omega_j=j/L,     j=0,...,M-1.\n\nThen\n\n    q(v)=sum_{j=0}^{M-1} exp(i omega_j v).\n\nWriting `x=v/L`,\n\n    q(v)=exp(i(M-1)x/2) * sin(Mx/2)/sin(x/2).\n\nOn J we have\n\n    x in [1-2theta,1-theta] subset (0,1),\n\nso the denominator stays uniformly away from zero:\n\n    |sin(x/2)| >= sin((1-2theta)/2)>0.\n\nTherefore\n\n    boxed:\n    |q(v)| <= c_theta := csc((1-2theta)/2)\n\nuniformly in M.\n\nHence\n\n    boxed:\n    integral_J |q(v)|^2 dv\n      <= theta L c_theta^2\n      = O_theta(L).\n\n## 2. The configuration respects the local-count scale\n\nThe ordinate spacing is `1/L`. Thus every unit interval contains at most `L+O(1)` points, exactly the shape permitted by\n\n    N(t,t+1] << log T\n\nwhen `L~log T`.\n\nChoose, for example,\n\n    M=floor(T L/4).\n\nThen the frequency span is\n\n    (M-1)/L < T/4,\n\nso the entire configuration fits inside a dyadic height window while still satisfying the unit-interval local-count scale.\n\nThe total positive multiplicity is\n\n    sum_j m_j = M ~ T L.\n\nConsequently\n\n    [integral_J |q|^2] / [sum_j m_j]\n      << 1/T.\n\nSince T grows faster than every fixed power of L, for every fixed C\n\n    1/T = o(L^(-C)).\n\nThus no inequality of the form\n\n    integral_J |q(v)|^2 dv\n      >= L^(-C) sum_j m_j\n\ncan hold uniformly under only positivity, equal/comparable depth, and the local zero-count input.\n\n## 3.
