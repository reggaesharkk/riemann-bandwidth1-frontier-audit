## 1. Rank-two generators of R

Therefore

    boxed:

      = (1/pi)(v u^T - u v^T).

So the complete off-diagonal resolvent is generated by the two vectors
u and v.

## 2. The current block

## 3. Product rank improves from four to three

Set

    X=BR,

    w = R alpha,
    s = B v,
    t = B u.

By the Leibniz rule,

    [D,X]
      = [D,B]R + B[D,R].

Substituting the two rank-two formulas and using R^T=R gives

    boxed:

          1 w^T
          + (s-alpha) u^T

    boxed:

The D2 Fourier channel therefore needs matrices of size at most 3 by 3.

## 4. One scalar squared-Cauchy commutator

Define the finite squared-Cauchy operator

    (K2 f)_i
      = sum_{j!=i} f_j/(i-j)^2,

and its commutator with the current scalar field,

    L_alpha

    (L_alpha f)_i

Because K2 is symmetric and M_alpha is diagonal,

    L_alpha^T = -L_alpha.
