# WP70-1A — averaged-shift fourth-moment lead

Date: 2026-10-04  
Status: **ACTIVE LEAD — NOT A THEOREM**

## Why this lead exists

The paper's Section 7.5(f) says that the missing fourth-trace input for useful
bandwidth (lambda>1/2) encodes Hardy--Littlewood-type additive correlations

[
sum_m (Lambda*Lambda)(m)(Lambda*Lambda)(m+h),
qquad |h|lesssim X^2/T.
]

It is natural to read this as a very hard fixed-shift problem.  But the trace
calculation does not obviously require a pointwise asymptotic for every single
(h).  The matrix trace arises after summing/integrating over the Gabor
indices, so what ultimately appears is a **weighted average over shifts**.

That difference may matter.

## Scale geometry

Write

[
X=T^lambda.
]

The off-diagonal fourth-moment shift range is

[
H:=X^2/T=T^{2lambda-1}.
]

Measured relative to (X),

[
H=X^{,2-1/lambda}.
]

Examples:

| lambda | shift exponent beta in H=X^beta |
|---:|---:|
| 0.80 | 0.750000 |
| 0.90 | 0.888889 |
| 0.95 | 0.947368 |
| 0.98 | 0.979592 |
| 1.00 | 1.000000 |

So the regime useful for a 70%+ fourth-moment attack is a **long shift
average**, not a microscopic/fixed-shift regime.

There is existing analytic-number-theory technology for average shifted
correlations over long ranges (for example the Matomäki--Radziwill--Tao
programme for von Mangoldt/divisor correlations).  Those theorems do **not**
directly prove what we need: our coefficients are (Lambda*Lambda), the
weight is the one generated by the trace expansion, and the required main
term/error normalization must match the zeta certificate exactly.

Nevertheless this suggests a concrete reduction target that is potentially
weaker than a full pointwise Hardy--Littlewood conjecture.

## Arithmetic decomposition lead

The generalized Mangoldt identity

[
Lambda_2(n)=Lambda(n)log n+(Lambda*Lambda)(n)
]

shows that the new coefficient is not arbitrary: it is the two-almost-prime
part of the second generalized von Mangoldt function.

This gives three possible analytic decompositions:

1. expand ((Lambda*Lambda))-correlations directly;
2. rewrite through (Lambda_2-Lambdalog) and exploit existing generalized
   Mangoldt / almost-prime estimates;
3. attack the weighted shift average by the circle method / dispersion rather
   than estimating each shift separately.

## Kill test

This route dies immediately if the exact trace expansion forces a
**pointwise-sign-sensitive** lower bound for each (h).  If only an averaged
signed sum survives, the route remains alive.

Therefore the next derivation is not a numerical search.  It is:

> Recover the exact (h)-weight in the (k=4) prime-side trace and determine
> whether average-shift control is logically sufficient.
