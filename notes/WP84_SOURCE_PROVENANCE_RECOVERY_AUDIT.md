## Hostile check against the requested conditions

No recovered candidate passes. The full-pool/root replay does certify the exact root identity and the uniform path-span bound (\le12000), so all paths have a root when (d'\ge12001). It does **not** choose the endpoints or give an actual row tuple. The signed near-product reconnaissance computes a centered candidate interval from q and d, but its own code explicitly marks the interval uncertified. The amplitude schema records R=6000 and all paths, but its exact source masks are delegated. The q-mask helper enforces its own q-domain truth table but cannot be passed to the full iterator and cannot certify other slot windows or selected rows. Diagnostic rank files fail the user’s explicit exclusions: they use small sections, surrogate logs, or R=1/2, and their own certificates refuse promotion. No candidate has a provenance hash corresponding to the missing selector because no such source file was found in any reachable history, reflog, branch, tag, packet section, attachment, or orphaned blob.

The available local formulas still preserve the full canceled-prime sum symbolically; they do not evaluate a source-selected matrix. Thus the full-pool and R=6000 requirements are stated, not enough to close the selector gap. (d'/d\to1) is also a family condition, not a rule determining (k_-(T),k_+(T)) at each T.

## Minimum missing source specification to reconstruct manually

Provide one pinned, source-authentic specification containing all of the following:

1. **Row set and order:** exact row IDs, their mathematical meaning (actual T/cell evaluations or coefficient jets), a deterministic ordered list for each of B6, B8a, B16, and B8b, and a mapping from each row to the 453 functional/layout IDs.
2. **Section selector:** exact rule (T\mapsto(k_-(T),k_+(T))), including endpoint trimming/tie-breaking and any dependence on carrier/path/cell; proof that the selected interval obeys the source conditions, in particular (d'/d\to1) and the needed large-section threshold.
3. **Full source mask:** executable χ predicate with ordinary-prime range, slot windows, equality and distinctness, carrier/alias admission, nonzero-detuning rule, orientation/conjugation, path closure, and section/root eligibility. Any precomputed state must be defined and derived from actual inputs rather than supplied as a Boolean oracle.
4. **Entry/jet mapping:** exact normalization and complete finite amplitude formula for every selected row/column, including all visible assignments, the complete hidden-q range and exclusions, R=6000 path phases, root sums, multiplicities, and the rule extracting each section-degree coefficient.
5. **Provenance:** original source filename/repository and immutable commit/blob hash (or a contemporaneous checked-in generator plus its pinned inputs) showing these are the actual theorem-selected rows and section, not a later diagnostic reconstruction.

Until this is supplied, stop before (K_B), (C_{m-1}), correction ranks, PNT on determinant combinations, or rank promotion. The exact replay/certificate artifact is `notes/WP84_SOURCE_PROVENANCE_RECOVERY_CERTIFICATE.json`; its validator is `src/wp84_source_provenance_recovery_replay.py`.
