## 1. Sharp partial translation on one Fourier band

Work on the physical interval

    I=[0,L]

with normalized Fourier basis

    e_k(x)=L^(-1/2) exp(i 2*pi*k*x/L),
    0<=k<d.

For 0<y<L define the positive non-cyclic partial shift

    (U_y^+ f)(x)
      = 1_[0,L-y](x) f(x+y).

Put

    s=y/L,
    D=diag(0,1,...,d-1),

and define the evaluation vector

    u_y
      = (1, exp(i2*pi*s), ..., exp(i2*pi*(d-1)*s))^T.

A direct integral gives, for k != l,

    (U_y^+)_{kl}
      = [u_y(k)-u_y(l)]/[i 2*pi (l-k)],

while

    (U_y^+)_{kk}
      = (1-s) u_y(k).

The diagonal value is irrelevant to the commutator with D.

Therefore

    boxed:
    [D,U_y^+]
      = i/(2*pi)
          [u_y 1^T - 1 u_y^T].

Hence

    boxed:
    rank [D,U_y^+] <= 2

for every shift y, exactly and at every finite d.

This is the continuous Fourier analogue of an LRSC boundary-orbit rank update.

## 2. Sum all prime blocks before taking the commutator

The sharp positive prime branch has the form

    boxed:

## 3. Hermitian prime operator remains rank two

The real prime operator is

    P_S=A_+ + A_+^*.

Because D is real diagonal,

    [D,A_+^*]=-[D,A_+]^*.

Writing

    r_S=q_S-conj(q_S)=2i Im(q_S),

    boxed:

      = i/(2*pi)
          [r_S 1^T - 1 r_S^T].

## 5. The resolvent inherits the same displacement rank

Let

    R_S(z)=(H_S-zI)^(-1),

for nonreal z.

The exact inverse commutator identity is

    [D,R_S]
      = -R_S [D,H_S] R_S.

    boxed:

    [D,R_S]
