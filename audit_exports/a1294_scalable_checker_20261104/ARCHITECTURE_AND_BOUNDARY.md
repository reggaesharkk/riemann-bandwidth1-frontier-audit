Recommended conversion format (DESIGN, not implemented): versioned arrays of canonical rational pairs (signed numerator, positive denominator, gcd=1); immutable checked catalog indexed by bounded naturals; boxes encoded by five pairs of dyadic endpoints; anchors as five u16 codes with denominator 16384; leaf payloads with fifteen model references, segment-list references, floors and an anchor; topology bytes 0=terminal, 1..5=split axis 0..4. Terminal kind and payload cursors must advance together. Reject out-of-range references, unknown kinds, noncanonical integers, absent children, trailing data, mismatched lengths, incomplete consumption and invalid interval coverage. Boxes should be derived by replay rather than repeated in payloads.

A conversion theorem must connect each compact payload to the rank model function, prove fifteen pointwise bounds and apply rankTangentCheck_energy_sound. Upstream replayAffineTree_sound and checkAffineTree already provide generic binary-tree soundness and exact final cursor checking. Reuse them instead of introducing a second tree proof system. Intern segment lists and rational values; do not emit one arithmetic theorem per row as the final architecture.

Cryptographic SHA-256/CRC checks authenticate external files against a declared archive. They do not establish the mathematical interpretation of bytes. Conversely, a kernel theorem about supplied literals does not authenticate their file provenance. A canonical conversion format needs an independently rerunnable converter, byte-for-byte output comparison, source hashes and eventually a Lean decoding/correspondence theorem.
