## WP19 mathematical result that survives on its own

For divergence-free Fourier fields and the specified (P_{11}) output projection, there are 5,574 output wavevectors. For each fixed (k), Cauchy–Schwarz gives
\[
|\widehat{P_{11}B(a,b)}(k)|\le \|a\|_{\ell^2}\|b\|_{\dot H^1}.
\]
Summing over the 5,574 outputs gives the source-stated factor \(\sqrt{5574}\). The v0.13 document then uses the Leray–Hopf bounds to prove \(\Gamma_M(u)\to\Gamma(u)\) in (L^1(0,T;L^2)). This implication is independent of the WP84 consumer, but it does not control the change in the Galerkin state between cutoffs. The missing state/backreaction term blocks the advertised all-(N) closure/regularity conclusion.
